# Attribution, copyright, and the disciplines this reader keeps

This reader was built with care to honor Paramahansa Yogananda's words and
Self-Realization Fellowship's stewardship of them. This document records that
posture so any future maintainer inherits the same reverence.

## Identity

- **What this is:** an independent, unofficial devotional reading companion that
  shows one entry per day from *The Spiritual Diary of Paramahansa Yogananda*.
- **What this is not:** an official Self-Realization Fellowship (SRF) product, a
  redistribution of SRF material beyond the daily-reading model, a commercial
  product, or a replacement for SRF's own publications and programs.
- **Source material:** *The Spiritual Diary* (Self-Realization Fellowship, Los
  Angeles, CA). All quoted writings remain © Self-Realization Fellowship.

## The disciplines (please keep these)

1. **No fabricated or machine-made scripture.** Yogananda's words are never
   scraped, OCR'd from unauthorized scans, paraphrased, or AI-generated. The
   missing days are filled **only** by faithful hand-transcription from the
   printed book (`docs/HAND_TRANSCRIPTION.md`). Fabricated attribution is a
   harder line than quotation and must never be crossed.
2. **No AI-generated sacred imagery.** SRF reveres only authentic images of the
   Gurus. This reader generates no sacred art and depicts no guru, deity, or
   religious figure. Any atmosphere is restrained, non-representational, and
   clearly not an official SRF image. (An earlier experimental AI-art feature was
   deliberately removed from this reader.)
3. **No monetization.** The reader is never sold, ad-supported, or tied to
   donations for content. Personal reflections stay on the reader's own device.
4. **No implication of official status.** SRF's name, marks, and emblem are used
   only nominatively for attribution. The app's lotus icon is an original,
   generic mark — no star, no lettering — and must not be replaced with anything
   that suggests official SRF endorsement unless SRF itself chooses to.

## Copyright posture

*The Spiritual Diary* is owned and published by Self-Realization Fellowship,
which stewards Yogananda's copyrights. This reader reproduces the daily readings
for personal, non-commercial, devotional use, in the spirit of the printed book.

**Donating the reader to SRF is the cleanest possible resolution of the
text-reproduction question:** once the rights-holder receives and (if it wishes)
accepts the gift, SRF governs the text and its presentation entirely. Until such
acceptance, the honest posture is to treat this as a private, personal
devotional tool with the plain "unofficial / not affiliated" disclosure that the
app already shows in its footer, About screen, and first-run welcome.
