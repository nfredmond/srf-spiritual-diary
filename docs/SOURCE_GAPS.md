### Search and verification notes

Searches used `from:SpiritualDiary_ since:YYYY-MM-DD until:YYYY-MM-DD`, with
Latest results. A missing result in one year did not establish that the account
lacked the reading. Earlier years supplied February 17, May 29, September 7,
September 22, September 25, October 4, and December 14. October 4 was absent from
the inspected 2021 through 2024 search results but appeared in 2020. December 14
was absent from the inspected 2021 through 2023 results but appeared in 2020.

Posting dates are not reading dates. For example, the September 15, 2024 post
linked above contains a September 16 card. Another September 17 post,
[1836226440171524484](https://x.com/SpiritualDiary_/status/1836226440171524484/photo/1),
contains a September 18 card and was excluded from the September 17 match.
These mismatches were detected by inspecting the images.

The leap-day card explicitly says February 29 and Humility. It is a separate
source lead, not evidence for substituting February 28. The May 14 card does not
identify its Bhagavad Gita translation. The October 4 card does not identify a
specific Para-gram. Those citation limits should remain explicit.

As a limited cross-check, the September 6, 2024 card,
[1832133499425562825](https://x.com/SpiritualDiary_/status/1832133499425562825/photo/1),
matches the existing September 6 quotation and Effort topic. One matching entry
does not authenticate the entire archive.

To resolve a bundled entry, record the edition or permitted source, transcribe
faithfully, retain the date/topic/author/book evidence, and reconcile the
unresolved-date list in the same change.
